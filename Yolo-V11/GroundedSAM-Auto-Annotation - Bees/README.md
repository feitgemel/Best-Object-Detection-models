Automate Your Dataset Creation with YOLO & Python! 🚀

Stop wasting hundreds of hours manually drawing boxes around every single bee! In this tutorial, I show you how to use Grounded-SAM and Autodistill to automatically annotate your dataset and train a state-of-the-art YOLO11 model to detect bees and flowers.

We take raw video footage of bees in the wild, extract high-quality frames using Supervision, and let AI do the "dirty work" of labeling with surgical precision. This is the ultimate "zero-effort" workflow for researchers, students, and developers building specialized computer vision models.

🔍 What You’ll Learn:

✅ Environment Setup: Conda, CUDA 12.8, and PyTorch 2.9.1. <br/> 
✅ Frame Extraction: Turning bee footage into training data. <br/>
✅ The AI Teacher: Using Grounded-SAM to auto-label bees and flowers. <br/>
✅ Verification: Visualizing AI-generated masks before training. <br/>
✅ Training: Powering through 300 epochs with YOLO11. <br/>
✅ Real-World Test: Running the final model on unseen bee footage. <br/>
